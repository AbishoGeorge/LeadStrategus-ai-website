Questions, answered plainly.

How is this different from a Clay or n8n course?

Those teach a tool. We teach you to design a system and choose tools by job, with frameworks from our own client campaigns.

Do I need to code?

No. You'll work with no-code and low-code tools; we show basic expressions where useful. Comfort with spreadsheets and browser tools is enough.

Will my engine be production-ready after 5 days?

You'll leave with a working, documented and tested engine. The 4-week implementation is where you run real sends and refine it; hardening it for full production use in your company is separate work.

Do you guarantee meetings or a job?

No. Results depend on your offer, market, data and execution. This is not a placement programme. We teach you to build and run the system that creates meetings.

Can my employer pay?

Yes. We issue a GST invoice and accept payment by Razorpay link or bank transfer against a PO. Registered businesses may be able to claim GST credit; please check with your CA.
