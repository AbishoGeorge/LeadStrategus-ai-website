Questions, answered plainly.

How is this different from a Clay or n8n course?

Those teach one tool. We teach you to design a system and pick the right tool for each job.

Do I need to code?

No. We use no-code and low-code tools. If you're comfortable with spreadsheets, you're ready.

Will my engine be ready to use after 5 days?

You'll leave with a working, tested engine. In the 4 weeks after, you run real sends and improve it.

Do you guarantee meetings or a job?

No. Results depend on your offer, market and execution. We teach you to build and run the system.

Can my employer pay?

Yes. We give a GST invoice and accept Razorpay or bank transfer against a PO.
